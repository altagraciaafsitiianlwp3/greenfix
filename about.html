About GreenFix

GreenFix is a tiny, independent project that helps people adopt sustainable habits without guilt or gimmicks. We focus on measurable impact, clear steps, and easy wins.

Our principles

  • Evidence first: prioritize peer‑reviewed findings and reputable data.
  • Progress over perfection: small steps beat stalled ideals.
  • Accessible: tips for renters, families, students, and busy folks.

What you’ll find here

Starter checklists, action‑ready guides, and curated resources to help you begin today.
