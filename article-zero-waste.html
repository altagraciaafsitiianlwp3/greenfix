Zero‑Waste Beginners Guide

10‑minute read · Progress, not perfection

Mindset shifts

  • Refuse freebies and single‑use items you don’t need.
  • Reuse containers; repair before replacing.
  • Recycle right—check local rules, not generic labels.

Starter swaps

  • Reusable bottle, cup, and tote bag.
  • Bulk staples (grains, nuts) using jars or bags.
  • Compost food scraps where possible.
