Cut Your Home Energy Use

8‑minute read · Updated quarterly

Quick wins (today)

  • Set heating to 19–20°C; lower water‑heater to 49–55°C.
  • Use smart power strips for TV/console/chargers.
  • Air‑dry clothes; wash at 30°C.

Low‑cost upgrades

  • LED bulbs everywhere (look for 2700–3000K for warm light).
  • Weather‑strip doors and seal window gaps.
  • Insulate hot‑water pipes and attic access.

Track & iterate

Log meter readings weekly; aim for 10–20% reduction over three months.
