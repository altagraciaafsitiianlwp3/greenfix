Travel Lighter, See More

7‑minute read · Plan smarter

Before you book

  • Prefer trains or nonstop flights when feasible.
  • Pick lodgings that publish energy/water policies.
  • Travel in shoulder seasons to reduce strain.

On the ground

  • Walk, cycle, or use public transport.
  • Eat local, seasonal food; avoid food waste.
  • Bring a small trash bag; leave places cleaner.
